<!-- Managed by backlog-workflow 1.6.0 -->

Interview the user about every relevant branch of the decision until shared
understanding is reached. Resolve dependencies between decisions one by one and
provide a recommended answer for each question.

Ask one question at a time and wait for feedback. Look up facts available from
the repository or environment; put product decisions to the user. Do not act on
the result until the user confirms shared understanding.
